# Counselor Meeting — Question List

Use this for the first real sit-down with the school counselor. Goal of this
meeting: get the actual course catalog/AP ladder, confirm the current
schedule is sustainable, and start the relationship early rather than in
junior year when it's transactional.

## Course Catalog & Rigor
- Can I get a copy of the full Program of Studies / course catalog, including
  every AP, IB, Honors, and CTE course the school offers by department?
- What is the honors/AP pathway in each core subject (English, Math, Science,
  History, World Language) from 9th through 12th grade here specifically?
- Is there a recommended or typical sequence, or do students design their own
  progression?
- How many AP/IB courses does a typical highly-competitive applicant from
  this school take by graduation, and is there a cap on how many a student
  can take per year?
- Does the school offer dual enrollment with a nearby college or university?
  If so, how does a student qualify and when do they start?
- Is there a CTE/elective pathway relevant to [student's interest — e.g.
  business, art, engineering]?

## Current Schedule Check-In
- The student is currently taking [list current courses] — is this a typical
  or heavy load compared to peers?
- Is there anything about this combination you'd flag as a concern,
  workload-wise, based on how the year is going so far?
- How do you recommend we monitor whether the pace is sustainable versus
  something to adjust for next year?

## Extracurriculars & Activities
- Does the school have a [club/team relevant to the student's interest, e.g.
  Mock Trial, robotics, debate]? If not, what's the process for a student to
  start a new club, and is there a staff advisor who might sponsor one?
- What does the leadership/officer pathway look like over 4 years for [key
  club/activity]?
- Are there teacher or staff contacts for [key clubs] who'd be good for the
  student to build a relationship with early, since those relationships often
  turn into recommendation letters later?
- Does the school track or support students seeking internships, shadowing
  opportunities, or volunteer placements?

## Testing
- Does the school administer the PSAT 8/9 to freshmen, and if so, when?
- When is PSAT 10 typically administered, and how are results shared with families?
- Does the school provide any SAT/ACT prep resources, fee waivers, or
  in-school test administration information I should know about now?

## Recommendation Letters & Counselor Relationship
- How far in advance do you recommend requesting the counselor letter of
  recommendation, and what's the best way to keep you updated on the
  student's activities and growth over the next few years so that letter is
  well-informed?
- Is there a system (e.g. Naviance or a similar platform) the school uses for
  college application tracking, and when does access typically start?
- How often do you recommend meeting — is an annual check-in enough, or would
  you suggest more frequent contact given the student's goals?

## Financial Aid & Local Opportunities
- Are you aware of any local scholarships or programs worth tracking early
  (e.g. local scholarship funds, a state Governor's School nomination, Boys
  State/Girls State)?
- Does the school nominate or support students for any state-run selective
  summer program, and when does that process start?

## Wrap-Up
- Based on everything I've shared about the student's interests, is there
  anything specific to this school or district that you'd recommend they
  look into that we haven't mentioned?
- What's the best way to reach you with quick questions between our check-ins?
